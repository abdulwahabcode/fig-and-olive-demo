Our Story

A little corner of calm on George Street

Fig & Olive began with a simple idea: modern lunch classics and elegant brunch dishes, made from scratch every morning and served somewhere beautiful. Our bright, intimate room seats just 36 guests, with tall windows looking out over the street toward Loch Ryan.

We've become a community anchor — a place for slow mornings, long lunches and well-earned cake. Families are always welcome, and four-legged friends too: we're proudly dog-friendly.
